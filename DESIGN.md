# Overview
A quiet working document for business owners and fractional CTOs. The interview sits beside the live RFP, like two pages on a desk.

# Colors
Warm paper, charcoal type, green actions, rust for unresolved questions.

# Typography
System sans for forms; Georgia headings in the document preview. Long text stays readable at 65 characters.

# Layout
Two columns on desktop, stacked on mobile. One interview round at a time, visible progress, editable answers and a persistent preview.

# Elevation & Depth
Fine borders and a light document shadow.

# Shapes
Small radii; no decorative dashboard tiles.

# Components
Labelled text areas, text buttons, native controls, status text, ordered sections.
